# Limitations

Freddie Mac's sample loan-level files do not represent all mortgage collateral and source fields can be missing, revised, or subject to reporting conventions. Historical performance does not predict future results.

The models are research baselines; they require sufficient observed forward history and temporal validation. Stress scenarios are simple multipliers, not agency methodologies or cash-flow projections. StrataCredit does not provide investment, legal, tax, or rating advice.
